INTERCONNECT

Where my research lives

Collective operations like Allgather and Allreduce move data between every node at once. How fast they run depends on the network topology, how many ports each node has, and the message size.

I design collective algorithms that adapt to the hardware instead of assuming one shape, and use calibrated LogGP models to choose their parameters without brute-force tuning.

COMPUTE NODES · CPU + GPU

One cluster, heterogeneous hardware

CPU racks above the interconnect, GPU racks below it, all tied together by a multi-level network. Modern supercomputers are heterogeneous like this, and that is why my work exists: communication tuned for one kind of hardware runs poorly on the next.

A NAIRR Pilot award gave my GPU collectives work 60,000 GPU hours across three supercomputers, plus $25K in AWS resources.
